# Venue policy snapshot

Checked on 2026-08-14 against the official [MLForSys 2026 Call for Papers](https://mlforsystems.org/call_for_papers.html).

- The main paper is limited to four pages, excluding references and appendices.
- An appendix is optional, and reviewers are not required to read it.
- Submissions do not have to be anonymized.

Consequence for this artifact: every claim necessary to judge the paper must remain in the four-page paper. This package is optional audit and reproduction support. Although anonymity is not required, machine-local paths, runtime-session locators, account identifiers, credentials, and restricted raw records are still removed for privacy and safe distribution.

The venue page is live policy and should be rechecked immediately before submission.
