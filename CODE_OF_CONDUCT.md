# Community conduct

SGE welcomes rigorous, good-faith technical discussion.

Participants should:

- critique evidence and code without attacking people;
- state uncertainty and conflicts of interest;
- preserve privacy and responsible-disclosure boundaries;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and deliberate disruption;
- correct unsupported claims when stronger evidence becomes available.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when needed to protect contributors or the research record. Conduct concerns can be reported privately through the repository's security-advisory channel when a public report would expose sensitive information.
